# Part 1 -- Prepare Chapter 1 Content

## A. Water Quality Report

Write a short report (approximately **150--250 words**) that the player
will read at the beginning of Chapter 1.

Include: 
- Current water quality
- Main environmental challenge
- One or two supporting facts
- A brief summary of the situation

*example fomat:*
| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Current Water Quality | Describe the current condition of the canal or lake. |
| Main Environmental Challenge | What is the biggest problem? |
| Supporting Facts | Include 1–2 facts or observations. |
| Summary | Briefly summarize the situation. |

> The report does not need to be completely factual. It can be
> simplified to better fit the gameplay.

- Goal

> Suggested stakeholders:
>
> - Factory Owner

>
> The two stakeholders should present **different perspectives**. Avoid making one character obviously "right" or "wrong." Both should have reasonable arguments that encourage the player to think critically.

## C. Policy Options

Design **three policy options**.

For each option, include: 
- Policy title
- Short description
- One benefit
- One trade-off

*example:*
| Policy | Benefit | Trade-off |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Approve Expansion | Creates more jobs | Increases pollution |
| Approve with Environmental Regulations | Balances development | Higher implementation cost |
| Reject Expansion | Protects water quality | Slows economic growth |
